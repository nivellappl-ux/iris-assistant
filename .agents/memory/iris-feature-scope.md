---
name: IRIS feature scope
description: Which IRIS features remain available and how removed local productivity records must be handled.
---

IRIS's active user-facing features are conversation, voice, settings, help, Mochi, and the Dynamic Island. Task, inbox/capture, project, and focus screens and actions were removed at the user's request. Existing local records must remain stored under their existing keys; do not clear, migrate, or send them to a provider.

**Why:** The user chose removal from the application, not merely from the Dynamic Island, and explicitly required preservation of existing local data.

**How to apply:** Do not restore these screens, shortcuts, commands, timers, or destructive controls unless the user asks. Keep the existing records untouched and isolated from AI requests.
